# Launch checklist

## Product
- Critical journeys tested on target devices; error monitoring live; rollback plan.
- Onboarding and empty states polished.

## Analytics
- Events for each funnel step; dashboards for activation and retention; UTM conventions.

## Legal and trust
- Privacy policy, terms, cookie consent (GDPR in the EU), data processing agreements, contact info.

## Marketing assets
- Landing page, demo video/GIFs, screenshots, press kit, launch post, FAQ.

## Distribution (pick what fits the ICP)
- Communities (Reddit, Indie Hackers, niche forums, Discord/Telegram), Product Hunt, Hacker News (Show HN), newsletters, partners, personal network, LinkedIn/X, SEO content.

## After launch
- Reply to every comment the first 48h, collect feedback, daily metrics review for a week, retro.

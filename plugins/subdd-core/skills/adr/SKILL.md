# Architecture Decision Records

Store in `docs/adr/NNNN-short-title.md`, numbered sequentially. Never edit an accepted ADR's decision; supersede it with a new one.

```markdown
# NNNN. <Title>

Date: YYYY-MM-DD
Status: proposed | accepted | superseded by NNNN

## Context
Forces at play: requirements, constraints, team, deadlines.

## Options considered
1. Option A: pros, cons.
2. Option B: pros, cons.

## Decision
What we chose, in one or two sentences.

## Consequences
What becomes easier, what becomes harder, follow-up work.
```
